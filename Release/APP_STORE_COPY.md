## Listing

**Name:** Helichopter

**Subtitle:** Fly at your own pace

**Promotional text:** Guide a helicopter through the gaps with adjustable controls, gentle practice, and room to find your own rhythm. Play offline on iPhone and iPad.

**Keywords:** helicopter,flying,arcade,practice,relaxing,switch,hover,obstacles

**Description:**

Find your rhythm in a helicopter obstacle game you can adjust to suit your play.

Fly through the gaps, try another round, or keep practicing with No-Fail Mode. Start with Gentle, Standard, or Challenge, then adjust gap size, pipe speed, and hitbox forgiveness.

Choose how you fly:
- Tap to Flap
- Hold to Hover
- Auto Hover with altitude nudges
- Two-Switch up/down controls

Make the experience comfortable:
- No-Fail Mode lets you keep going after collisions.
- Calm Mode removes collision sounds and impact vibration.
- Show or hide the score, and switch music and sound effects on or off.
- Adjust background scrolling and choose among three themes and six colour palettes.
- Replay How to Play whenever you need a reminder.

Menus and Settings offer scalable text and switch scanning. VoiceOver provides flight actions and spoken gap guidance. During keyboard or controller play, hold the primary switch to pause, then release it before choosing a menu item. Settings let you adjust the hold delay and scanning speed.

Helichopter works offline without an account, ads, or in-app purchases. Settings and scores are saved on your device.

**What's New (2.0.1, draft):**

Smoother screen changes. Moving between Home, Settings, and a game now fades one whole screen into the next, so two menus never appear at the same time. Taps and switch presses wait until the new screen is ready, and VoiceOver announces each screen once. With Reduce Motion on, the fade is shorter.

**What's New (2.0, as submitted):**

This update adds adjustable flight controls, switch-scanning menus and Settings, VoiceOver flight actions and gap guidance, scalable text, colour themes, No-Fail and Calm modes, a replayable guide, refreshed helicopter animation, and a settings reset that preserves scores.

## Review notes

No login, purchase, server connection, or special account is needed. The first-run guide can be completed or skipped; How to Play on the home screen reopens it.

1. Open Settings. Choose Gentle for slower play. No-Fail Mode is on by default; turn it off to reach Round Over after a collision.
2. For keyboard-emulating switch input, Space or Enter selects. In Settings → Switch Access, Auto-Scan Menus starts timed scanning; Menu Scan Mode → Two-Switch uses secondary input (2) to advance and primary input to select. Sliders open Decrease/Increase/Done choices.
3. During flight, hold the primary keyboard/controller switch for 3 seconds to pause. Release before selecting Resume or Home. The delay is adjustable from 2–10 seconds.
4. With VoiceOver enabled, focus Helicopter flight control. Double-tap flaps, toggles hover, nudges up, or toggles rising according to the selected flight scheme. The Move down custom action provides downward control where applicable. Pause or accessibility escape opens the pause menu. Guidance is periodic, not continuous.
5. Settings → Reset Settings → Restore Defaults restores Standard difficulty, default controls/theme/audio, while retaining scores and guide completion. Cancel leaves settings intact. Lock Settings must be off to reset.

## Accessibility statement

Helichopter includes scalable menu and Settings text, spoken switch-scanning labels, four flight control schemes, adjustable scanning and pause timing, VoiceOver flight actions and periodic gap guidance, contrasting object boundaries, colour palettes, Reduce Motion handling, and optional collision sounds/vibration.

These features are implemented. Full sound-only play and physical assistive-device compatibility have not yet been established. Avoid claims that the game supports every disability or guarantees independent play for every player. Accessibility Nutrition Labels (published in App Store Connect 2026-10-08): Larger Text, Dark Interface, Differentiate Without Color Alone, Sufficient Contrast, Reduced Motion. VoiceOver and Voice Control are held until device testing. Criteria: [Apple's criteria](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/overview-of-accessibility-nutrition-labels/).
