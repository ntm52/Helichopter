Privacy Policy

Helichopter for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 1, 2026

Helichopter is an offline helicopter game. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, third-party analytics, or tracking.

Information the app stores

The app stores your gameplay settings, your scores, and whether you have completed the introductory guide. This information stays on your device and is used only to remember your preferences and progress. The app never connects to the internet and never sends this information to the developer or anyone else.

Your device's backups may include this app data, according to your Apple device settings.

Deleting your information

Settings → Reset Settings restores the default preferences and keeps your saved scores and guide completion. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Backup copies are managed through your device and backup settings.

Children

Helichopter collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contacting support

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to respond to you. Please don't include sensitive personal information.

Apple services

The App Store and device backups are provided by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Changes

This policy describes the current app. If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
