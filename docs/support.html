Support

Helichopter for iPhone and iPad

Getting started

Choose Play to begin, or How to Play for the guide. The helicopter waits for your first input before pipes start appearing. Settings offers Gentle, Standard, and Challenge difficulty.

Making play more comfortable

No-Fail Mode lets you keep flying after collisions. Calm Mode removes collision sounds and vibration. You can set Background Scroll to Still, turn music and sound effects on or off, hide the score, and change colours and themes.

Switch controls

Space or Enter starts scanning or selects the highlighted item. With timed scanning, wait until the item you want is highlighted, then select it. With Two-Switch scanning, press 2 to move to the next item and your primary switch to select. Selecting a slider opens Decrease, Increase, and Done.

During play, hold your primary switch for 3 seconds to pause, then release it before choosing a menu item. If you hold the switch to hover, increase Hold Switch to Pause in Settings.

VoiceOver and larger text

During flight, focus "Helicopter flight control" and double-tap to fly; use its actions to move down or pause. The escape gesture also pauses. Spoken gap guidance is given every few seconds. Text follows Larger Text in iOS Accessibility settings.

Restoring settings

Open Settings → Reset Settings. Cancel keeps your setup; Restore Defaults returns to Standard difficulty and the default controls, theme, and audio. Scores and guide completion are kept. If Settings is locked, turn off Lock Settings first.

Contact

Email helichopter.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. For accessibility issues, mention the input method or assistive feature you were using if you're comfortable sharing it.
